## 5. KPI formulas (implement consistently)
VIP annual target = meeting_frequency * periods_per_year, with QUARTER=4, HALF_YEAR=2, YEAR=1. Example: frequency=2 and HALF_YEAR => annual target=4. Preserve any existing frequency/period aliases via normalization; do not silently reinterpret values.

Agency annual target = sum of annual targets of eligible VIPs in agency.
Agency completed = sum over VIPs of min(qualifying actual meetings for that VIP in the selected year, that VIP annual target), unless existing approved business logic specifies a different cap. Agency annual KPI (%) = 100 * agency completed / agency annual target. Do not allow extra meetings for one VIP to hide unmet targets of other VIPs. If annual target=0, show N/A and keep such agencies in a separate 'No target' state rather than incorrectly categorizing them as red.

Agency status based on annual KPI: RED <50%; YELLOW 50% to <70%; GREEN >=70%. Display the three counts and separately show N/A count if applicable, so totals reconcile. If business insists exactly three counts, flag this case for product decision; do not misclassify zero-target agencies.

Stakeholder completion rate = min(actual meetings, annual target)/annual target * 100. Show raw actual meeting count separately where helpful. For the historical average KPI, prefer a historical baseline stored in existing data if one exists. Otherwise calculate an explicitly labeled historical average over prior complete years with available data and disclose years used. If no valid historical baseline exists, display N/A; never use 61% from screenshot as a hardcoded value.

Meeting cadence/slot status (if used in detail views) must respect period boundaries: QUARTER => 4 periods; HALF_YEAR => 2; YEAR => 1. Allocate meeting occurrences to their actual periods; surplus in one period must not automatically satisfy another period. Past incomplete slots are overdue; current period can be in progress; future slots not yet due. Ensure annual aggregate and period-based display are explicitly differentiated and consistent with the approved KPI definitions. For YTD KPI, use only periods elapsed/current as specified by the existing History Report business logic, not full-year denominator without labeling.
