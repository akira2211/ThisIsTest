HUONG DAN GHEP VA GIAI NEN BSIG_UI.rar

Tai hai file sau ve cung mot thu muc:
  BSIG_UI.rar.part-000
  BSIG_UI.rar.part-001

Linux / macOS:

1. Ghep hai phan theo dung thu tu:
   cat BSIG_UI.rar.part-000 BSIG_UI.rar.part-001 > BSIG_UI.rar

2. Kiem tra SHA-256:
   Linux:  sha256sum BSIG_UI.rar
   macOS:  shasum -a 256 BSIG_UI.rar
   Ket qua mong doi:
   fb11e8cf036cb28b3ff6a34815291ce7efe3ae46241a0f432c688e211af2ee70

3. Giai nen bang ung dung ho tro RAR, hoac neu da cai unrar:
   unrar x BSIG_UI.rar

Windows PowerShell (chay trong thu muc chua hai phan):

1. Ghep theo dung thu tu:
   cmd /c copy /b BSIG_UI.rar.part-000+BSIG_UI.rar.part-001 BSIG_UI.rar

2. Kiem tra SHA-256:
   Get-FileHash BSIG_UI.rar -Algorithm SHA256
   Ket qua mong doi:
   fb11e8cf036cb28b3ff6a34815291ce7efe3ae46241a0f432c688e211af2ee70

3. Mo BSIG_UI.rar bang WinRAR hoac 7-Zip de giai nen.

Day la hai phan chia byte cua mot file RAR, khong phai hai RAR volume;
can ghep chung thanh BSIG_UI.rar truoc khi giai nen.
