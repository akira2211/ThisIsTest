2. Business Context

The system manages R&D asset requests across eight departments:

1. SW Engineering
2. System Platform
3. SQA
4. Product
5. AV Enterprise
6. Android
7. Monitor
8. Mac OS

There are four major request categories:

MRO Items

- Items requested for purchase by R&D teams.
- Include procurement and monthly budget management.
- This is the highest-priority business category.

Import Items

- Items shipped from headquarters or external locations.
- Track shipping, arrival, receiving, and completion.

LPR Items

- Items requested from existing factory warehouse inventory.
- Track availability, allocation, and delivery.

Exception Production Items

- Items requested for new production by factory production lines.
- Track approval, production, and completion.

MRO Budget is the most important financial feature, but all four categories must be represented in the Overview.

8. Data Architecture

Use JavaScript objects from "data.js" as the single source of truth.

Do not hardcode numerical metrics directly in HTML.

Derive KPI values, category totals, percentages, charts, and tables from the mock dataset.

Ensure mathematical consistency:

- Total Requests = sum of category counts.
- Request statuses must be mutually exclusive.
- Completed + In Progress + Pending + Overdue = Total Requests.
- Budget Available = Allocated - Actual Spent - Committed.
- Budget utilization = (Actual Spent + Committed) / Allocated.
- Department totals must match overall totals.

Support global filters:

- Department
- Time Period

All KPI cards, charts, and tables should update when filters change.

Use realistic sample data across all eight departments and all four request categories.
