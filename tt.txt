2. Apply the active highlight to that card.

4. Status Persistence

Meeting/contact status must be calculated from actual stored records, not from the currently selected month.

Rules:

- Completed records remain Completed when switching months.
- Planned records remain Planned until their completion conditions are satisfied.
- Historical completed meetings must never become Planned again simply because another month is selected.
- Do not reset or overwrite any meeting records when switching months.
- Reopening a previously selected month must display the same persisted results.

Calculate each month's completion based on the planned contacts and valid completed meeting records associated with that month.

Avoid double-counting duplicate meeting records.
